HALL-PAY

Terms of Service

These terms explain the basic rules for using HALL-PAY to access municipal services online.

Effective date: May 22, 2026 Municipality of Kabacan

Use of the Service

You may use HALL-PAY to register an account, submit applications, upload requirements, send payment proof, track status, download receipts and permits, and verify QR-coded documents.

Your Responsibilities

  • Provide true, complete, and current information.
  • Upload only documents you are authorized to submit.
  • Keep your login credentials private.
  • Do not misuse the system, bypass security, or submit fraudulent records.

Applications and Payments

Submission through HALL-PAY does not automatically guarantee approval. Municipal personnel may review, request corrections, reject, approve, or complete applications according to applicable rules and requirements.

Receipts, Permits, and QR Verification

Digital receipts and permits are generated from municipal records. QR verification is provided to help confirm authenticity, but official records remain subject to review by authorized municipal offices.

Availability

The system may be unavailable during maintenance, network issues, cloud service interruptions, or emergency conditions. The municipality may update, suspend, or change features when needed.

Account Access

Accounts may be limited, suspended, or reviewed if suspicious, abusive, inaccurate, or unauthorized activity is detected.

Contact

For service questions, contact hallpay@kabacan.gov.ph or visit the Municipal Hall, Kabacan, North Cotabato.
