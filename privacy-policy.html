HALL-PAY

Privacy Policy

HALL-PAY helps citizens access municipal services, submit applications, upload requirements, make payment submissions, and receive verified receipts and permits.

Effective date: May 22, 2026 Municipality of Kabacan

Information We Collect

We collect account details, contact information, application data, uploaded documents, payment references, support messages, device and usage information, and verification records needed to provide municipal services.

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your HALL-PAY account.
  • To process applications, assessments, payments, receipts, permits, and support requests.
  • To send service updates, reminders, announcements, and verification notices.
  • To audit activity, prevent misuse, and improve system reliability.

Document Uploads and Payments

Uploaded documents and payment proofs may be stored using trusted cloud services. Files are used only for application review, identity validation, payment confirmation, and record keeping.

Sharing

Information may be shared with authorized municipal personnel, service providers that operate the platform, and government offices when required to process requests or comply with applicable law.

Security

We use account authentication, access controls, audit records, and cloud storage safeguards. No online system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, so users should keep passwords private and report suspicious activity.

Your Choices

You may update your profile details, manage notification preferences, request support, and ask the municipality to review or correct records where allowed by policy and law.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hallpay@kabacan.gov.ph or visit the Municipal Hall, Kabacan, North Cotabato.
